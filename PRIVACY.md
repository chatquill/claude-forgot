# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

claude-forgot is a set of written instructions for Claude. It has no code, no servers, no MCP connectors and no analytics.

## What it handles

When you run it, the skill reads the conversation in your own Claude session to find the rules you set. It does not read your files, connectors or any other data.

## What it stores or sends

Nothing. The plugin does not store, log or send any data anywhere. Its author never receives your conversations or anything else from your session.

If an earlier message contains a password, API key, token, card number or other secret, the skill is told to leave it out of the rules block and tell you it was removed. Personal details that are not rules are left out too.

Your conversations are handled by Claude itself, under Anthropic's own privacy policy.
